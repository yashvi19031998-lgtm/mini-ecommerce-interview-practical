## 5. Post-Generation Assembly & Typography Strategy

AI video generators like Google Flow / Veo produce stunning motion and lighting, but micro-typography (like phone numbers and rupee symbols) can occasionally render with slight softness. Follow this professional workflow:

1. **Generate 6 Clips in Google Flow:**
   - Enter each scene prompt separately in 9:16 aspect ratio.
   - Set motion style to **Cinematic / Commercial**.
2. **Import into CapCut, Premiere Pro, or Canva:**
   - Stack the 6 clips consecutively (Total duration: 22–24 seconds).
   - Add **Flash / Light Leak / Whip Pan** transitions (0.2s duration) between scenes.
3. **Overlay Crisp Vector Typography:**
   - Use **Space Grotesk (Bold / 700)** for: `FESTIVAL CREATIVE PACKAGE`, `JUST ₹500 / MONTH`, `DM US TO SUBSCRIBE`.
   - Use **Inter (Medium / 500)** for: `10 Festival Images`, `1 Promotional Reel`, `+91 9106060400`.
   - Apply a subtle drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`) to ensure all text pops against festive particle effects.
4. **Audio Track:**
   - Pair with an upbeat, modern fusion festival track (Indian dhol rhythm blended with contemporary electronic beats).
