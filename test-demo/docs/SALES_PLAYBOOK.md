# Sangeeta Infotech — B2B Sales & Objection Handling Playbook
=============================================================

This playbook provides word-for-word closing scripts, objection handling frameworks, and pilot offers tailored for Sangeeta Infotech (dedicated developers @ ₹450/- INR / hour or $6–$8/hr).

---

## 1. The "No-Brainer" Pilot Offer: 20-Hour Discovery & Delivery Sprint

Instead of pitching a large, open-ended retainer upfront, pitch this low-risk starter pilot:

- **Price:** $160 – $200 total (or approx. ₹13,500 – ₹17,000 INR).
- **Turnaround:** 3 to 5 business days.
- **Deliverables:**
  1. 60-minute technical discovery & workflow breakdown.
  2. 1-Page Technical Roadmap (bottlenecks, recommended architecture, sprint milestones).
  3. Working Proof-of-Concept (POC) for one defined feature, portal screen, or API integration.
  4. Full source-code committed directly into the client's GitHub/GitLab repository.
  5. Short Loom video walkthrough & technical handover.
- **The Close:** 
  > *"If the code quality or communication isn't exactly what you expected, you can stop after the 20-hour sprint with zero further commitment."*

---

## 2. Objection Handling Scripts

### Objection 1: "Why should we hire developers from India at ₹450/hr instead of hiring locally?"
**Response Script:**
> *"That’s a fair question. The rate is only one part of the decision—what really matters is whether you get senior ownership, clear communication, and predictable delivery without adding permanent payroll.*
>
> *We work as a dedicated extension of your team: a named developer or pod, overlap with your working hours, weekly demos, source-code ownership in your repository, and a fixed-scope pilot before any long-term commitment.*
>
> *Local hiring is ideal when you need daily in-person collaboration or a permanent on-site executive. But for a defined build, modernization, or sprint capacity gap, our model lets you start immediately, scale down without severance, and keep full control of your codebase.*
>
> *Would a 20-hour fixed-scope pilot be a low-risk way to compare delivery quality before making a larger decision?"*

---

### Objection 2: "How do we know the code quality will be good?"
**Response Script:**
> *"Don't take our word for it—judge us on working code. We start with a bounded 20-hour sprint where our developers push code directly to your branch for your team's code review. You see our architecture, test coverage, and documentation firsthand before committing to a larger roadmap."*

---

### Objection 3: "What about the time zone difference?"
**Response Script:**
> *"Our engineers align a defined daily overlap window (3 to 4 hours) with your business hours for standups, sync calls, and Slack/Teams collaboration, while doing deep-focus development during the rest of the day. You wake up with tested pull requests ready for review."*

---

### Objection 4: "Who owns the Intellectual Property (IP) and code?"
**Response Script:**
> *"You own 100% of the IP, code, and assets from day one. We develop directly inside your GitHub/GitLab repository under your access controls, with mutual NDA protection."*
