# ₹500 Post Pack → IT Project: Outreach Playbook for Gujarat SMEs

20 Nov 2026 · For the 2-person BDE team and the owner, Sangeeta Infotech

**The bottom line:** the offer is right; the execution plan is the risk. A ₹500 entry product works with Gujarat SMEs, but only when **WhatsApp and the phone lead and e-mail supports**, when the hook is a **free sample with their logo**, and when the list is **local businesses, not IT buyers**. Done that way, 50–100 paid clients in month 1 is reachable; by e-mail alone it is not (expect 5–15).

> **Data check from our own system (20 Nov):** of 3,123 leads, most are job posts, freelance projects and IT companies. Only **788** are in Gujarat, **257** with a phone number. These are the wrong audience for a post pack. Week 1 starts with building a new local SME list (section 2, days 1–3).

---

## 1. Honest critique of the current e-mail

**Score: 5 / 10.** Clear price, low risk and "ready on WhatsApp" are real strengths. Six things will hold the reply rate at roughly 0.5–1%.

| # | Problem | Why it hurts with Gujarat SMEs | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **E-mail is the main channel** | Shop owners and MSME owners rarely read `info@` mail; they live on WhatsApp and the phone. Many SMEs have no working e-mail at all. | E-mail is touch #2. The first touch is a WhatsApp or a phone call. |
| 2 | **No proof** | A stranger selling ₹50 posts sounds like low quality or a scam. | Show 2–3 real sample posts from their category (clinic, salon, jeweller…) as a link or image, plus "made for 40+ Ahmedabad businesses" only when true. |
| 3 | **Weak hook: price first** | "10 posts ₹500" competes with Canva and the owner's nephew. | Lead with a **free sample with their own logo and shop name**. Paying comes after they see it. |
| 4 | **Spam signals** | ALL-CAPS heading, 🚀 emoji bullets, price in the subject line and two offers → Promotions tab or spam. | Plain text, one offer, no caps, short subject without the price. |
| 5 | **"Hi Team {Company Name}" and a generic question** | It reads as a mass mail. | Use the owner's or shop name and one line that is true for their business (category, area, upcoming festival). |
| 6 | **No reason for the low price, no deadline** | "Why so cheap?" kills trust; without a reason to act, they postpone. | "Intro price for the first 30 businesses in Ahmedabad this month", tied to the next festival (wedding season, Christmas / New Year, **Uttarayan 14 Jan**). |

**Rewritten e-mail (plain text, about 70 words):**

```
Subject: {Shop name} na festival posts – free sample?

Namaste {Owner name / Team},

Uttarayan ane wedding season mate {city} na {category} businesses
WhatsApp ane Instagram par roj posts muke che.

Ame tamara logo ane shop name sathe 2 sample posts free banavi
aapishu – game to 10 posts no pack fakt ₹500 (intro offer, aa mahine).

Sample joiye? Aa mail no reply "YES" karo ke WhatsApp karo:
https://wa.me/919106060400

– {BDE name}, Sangeeta Infotech, Ahmedabad
```

Send it in English for non-Gujarati businesses; keep the same structure.

## 2. 30-day roadmap for a 2-person BDE team

### Target math (work backwards from 75 paid clients)

| Step | Rate (Gujarat SME, WhatsApp + call) | Month 1 count |
|---|---|---|
| Businesses contacted (WhatsApp + call + e-mail) | — | **2,500–3,000** |
| Interested ("send sample") | 8–10% | **~250** |
| Sample delivered within 24 h | 95% | ~240 |
| Paid ₹500 | 30–35% of samples | **75–85** |

That is **60 personal touches per BDE per working day** (calls + WhatsApps), plus the system's e-mails. With fewer touches the plan will not reach 50.

### Days 1–3: set-up (do not skip)

1. **Build the list (500+ businesses before day 4).** Pick 3 niches that post a lot and have money: **clinics / dentists, salons and spas, gyms, boutiques and jewellers, restaurants and cafés, coaching classes, real-estate agents, furniture and electronics showrooms**. Sources: Google Maps, IndiaMART and the Gujarat directories in our app (Lead Sources). Keep only businesses with a mobile number.
2. **Sample kit per niche:** 5 ready templates per niche (festival, offer, testimonial, "new arrival", "open today") so a sample takes **10 minutes** with their logo. Use Canva or our AI creative tools.
3. **Payment:** UPI QR + payment link; GST invoice template.
4. **Delivery SOP:** brief form (logo, phone, 3 offers), delivery in 24 h, 1 free revision.
5. **WhatsApp Business** on the office number: catalog with the ₹500 and ₹1,000 packs, quick replies (section 3), labels (New, Sample sent, Paid, Upsell).
6. **Tracking:** every contact logged in the app (Leads → lead panel → "I called / I sent a WhatsApp"); the WhatsApp desk gives each BDE their daily list.

### Daily routine per BDE (Mon–Sat)

| Time | Work | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00–10:30 | Review yesterday's replies; send samples promised | 0 pending |
| 10:30–13:00 | **Calls**: 30-second pitch → "Can I WhatsApp you 2 free samples?" | 35 calls |
| 14:00–15:30 | **WhatsApp desk**: first messages and follow-ups | 25 messages |
| 15:30–17:00 | Make and send samples; close payments | 8–10 samples |
| 17:00–18:30 | Deliver paid packs; upsell check-ins (section 4) | all on time |
| Saturday | Referrals: ask every paid client for 2 names | 10 referrals |

**30-second call script:** "Namaste, {name} ji? Hu {BDE} Sangeeta Infotech, Ahmedabad thi. Tamara {shop} mate Uttarayan / season na 2 posts tamara logo sathe **free** banavi ne WhatsApp karu? Game to j aagal vadhvanu. Aa j number par WhatsApp chhe ne?"

### Week by week

| Week | Focus | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (days 4–7) | Test 3 niches, 2 versions of the pitch | 60 samples, 15 paid; pick the best niche |
| 2 | Double down on the best 2 niches; add the reel pack to every paid client | 35 paid in total |
| 3 | Festival push (Uttarayan / New Year packs) + referrals | 60 paid in total |
| 4 | Upsells (section 4), case studies, testimonials | 75+ paid; 3–5 website deals in the pipeline |

### KPIs to watch every evening

Touches per BDE (60) · samples sent (8–10 each) · sample → paid (30%+) · delivery in 24 h (100%) · paid → upsell call booked (20%+).

**Capacity warning:** 75 clients × 10 posts = **750 posts in a month**. Have a designer (or an AI-assisted template pipeline) ready before day 4, or delivery will break and refunds will follow.

## 3. WhatsApp nudges for leads who did not reply within 24 hours

**Day 1 (after the e-mail)**

## 4. Upsell playbook: ₹500 post pack → ₹15,000–₹50,000 project in 14 days

The rule: **earn the right first**. Deliver on time and look at their business before you sell anything.

| Day | Action | Script (Gujlish; translate as needed) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Deliver early, 1 free revision | "Tamara 10 posts taiyar che ✅ Koi pan ma ferfar joiye to kaho, free." |
| 2 | Results check, ask for a testimonial | "Posts muki? Customers taraf thi kayo post saro chalyo? Ek line no feedback aapsho to amne madad thashe 🙏" |
| 4 | **Free 5-minute online audit** (Google Business profile, website, WhatsApp catalog) sent as 3 bullet points | "Tamaro Google profile joyo – 3 nani vastu sudhare to vadhu calls aavi shake: 1) … 2) … 3) … Aa free sudharva ma madad karu?" |
| 7 | **Offer a tier that fits what you saw** | "Ghana customers Google par shodhe che ane tamari website nathi. Ame {category} mate 7 divas ma website banavi chhie – ₹14,999 thi. Aavta athvadiye 10 minute vaat kariye?" |
| 10 | Proof: case study of a similar local business | "{Similar shop} ni website pachhi WhatsApp enquiries vadhi. Screenshot mokalu chhu." |
| 14 | Decision with a reason to act now | "Aa mahina na offer ma website sathe 3 mahina na posts free che – shukravar sudhi. Shu kahevu che?" |

**Tiers to offer (pick by what the audit shows):**

| Need you saw | Offer | Price band |
|---|---|---|
| No website / only a Facebook page | Business website + Google profile set-up | ₹15,000–₹25,000 |
| Takes orders on WhatsApp / phone | Online catalog / e-commerce with WhatsApp ordering | ₹25,000–₹45,000 |
| Many enquiries, no follow-up system | CRM / booking / billing software | ₹40,000–₹1,00,000 |
| Happy with the posts | Monthly posting retainer (stepping stone) | ₹2,500–₹5,000 / month |

Target: 20% of paid clients take an upsell call; 25% of those buy → about **4–5 projects from 75 clients** in the first 6 weeks, plus retainers.

## 5. The top 3 objections, with rebuttals

**1. "Amare jarur nathi, customers already aave che." (We don't need it.)**
> "Saras che ke customers aave che 👍 Etle j aa vaat: je customer ek vaar aavyo, tene WhatsApp par festival offer jaay to fari aave. 2 posts free banavi aapu chhu – kaam na laage to delete kari dejo."
*Why it works:* it agrees, moves from "new customers" to "repeat customers", and removes the risk.

**2. "Amaro designer che / Canva thi jate banavie chhie." (We do it ourselves.)**
> "Ekdum saru. Ame tamara designer ni jagya nathi letaa – busy season ma jyare samay na hoy, tyare 10 ready posts ₹500 ma, ek post na ₹50. Tamaro ek kalak bachi jaay. Ek sample sarkhavi juo?"
*Why it works:* respects their effort, sells time, invites comparison instead of an argument.

**3. "Pehla kaam batavo / online paisa nahi aapu." (Trust: show work first, no advance.)**
> "Barabar che, etle j pehla 2 posts **free** – tamara logo sathe. Game to j ₹500, ane te pan UPI par GST bill sathe. Ahmedabad ma amaru office che, {address}; ruba-ru pan aavi shako."
*Why it works:* the free sample answers it before money is mentioned, and a local office plus GST bill answers "scam".

**Bonus – "Pachhi vaat kariye / send details." (The stall.)**
> "Jarur. Details karta sample vadhu kaam kare – aaje sanje tamara logo sathe 1 post mokli daun, 2 minute joi lejo. Kayo logo vaparu, WhatsApp DP vado?"
*Why it works:* converts a vague "later" into a small, specific yes.

### Compliance notes (keep the number and the domain safe)

- WhatsApp: personal, varied messages, ~25 new chats a day per number, office hours, stop on "no". No bulk-sender software.
- E-mail: one mail per company (the app enforces it), an easy way to say no, no buying of lists.
- Calls: respect DND requests; never call after 8 pm.
